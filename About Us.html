Who We Are

Back in early 2025, a small group of journalists and tech enthusiasts sat around a coffee table in Assam , frustrated with how difficult it had become to find news that was not only fast but also trustworthy. That frustration turned into an idea, and that idea eventually became mega-store.in. We started with nothing more than a laptop, a shared hosting plan, and a stubborn belief that readers deserve better than clickbait headlines and recycled press releases.

Today, we have grown a full-fledged digital newsroom with contributors spread across India, the UK, the US, and Southeast Asia. But despite the growth, we have not forgotten why we started this in the first place. Every article that goes live on our platform is still reviewed by a real editor, fact-checked against multiple sources, and written with the reader in mind, not an algorithm.

Our Mission

We believe journalism should serve the public, not advertisers or political agendas. Our mission is straightforward: give people the information they need to understand the world around them, without dressing it up in sensationalism or burying it behind paywalls. We want our readers to walk away from every article feeling genuinely informed, not manipulated.

Too many news sites today chase traffic at the expense of truth. We have made a conscious decision to do the opposite. Yes, it means we publish fewer stories than some of our competitors. But it also means that when you read something on mega-store.in, you can trust that it has been put together with care, accuracy, and a genuine respect for your time.

What We Cover

Our editorial team covers a wide range of topics because we know our readers have diverse interests. Here is what you can expect from each section:

  • Politics: We dig into policy changes, election coverage, and political developments with a focus on facts rather than partisan spin. Whether it is a state election in India or a geopolitical shift in Europe, we break down what is actually happening and why it matters to you.
  • Technology: From smartphone launches to AI regulation debates, our tech coverage aims to explain complex topics in plain English. We test products when possible, interview industry insiders, and avoid the hype that often surrounds Silicon Valley announcements.
  • Sports: Cricket, football, tennis, athletics, you name it. Our sports desk covers match reports, player interviews, and tournament analysis. We focus on stories that go beyond the scoreboard, looking at the human side of competition.
  • Entertainment: Movie reviews, celebrity interviews, music releases, and cultural commentary. We call out bad films when we see them, and we celebrate genuinely creative work without being swayed by studio PR.
  • Business: Stock market movements, startup stories, economic policy, and corporate developments. Our business coverage is written for everyday readers, not finance professionals, so you do not need an MBA to understand it.
  • World: International news with context. We explain the background behind major global events so you are not left wondering why something is happening.
  • Health: Medical breakthroughs, wellness advice, mental health awareness, and public health updates. We consult medical professionals before publishing health content and always distinguish between established science and emerging research.

How We Work

Our newsroom operates on a simple principle: verify first, publish second. When a story breaks, our first instinct is not to rush it live. It is to confirm the facts, reach out to primary sources, and make sure we are not repeating unverified claims. This sometimes means we are a few minutes slower than other outlets, but we think accuracy is worth the wait.

We also maintain a strict separation between our editorial and advertising teams. Advertisers do not influence what we cover, how we cover it, or whether we cover it at all. If we review a product or service, we disclose any relationships upfront. If we make a mistake, we correct it publicly and transparently.

Our writers come from a mix of backgrounds. Some have decades of experience at major newspapers. Others are fresh voices who bring new perspectives. What they all share is a commitment to honest reporting and a refusal to treat readers like clicks on a dashboard.

Our Values

Accuracy Above Speed: In an age where everyone wants to be first, we would rather be right. Every claim in our articles is backed by evidence, and we update stories as new information becomes available.

Editorial Independence: No investor, advertiser, or political figure tells us what to write. Our editorial decisions are made by journalists, not business executives.

Transparency: When we get something wrong, we admit it. When we have a conflict of interest, we disclose it. When we use AI tools for research, we say so clearly.

Reader Respect: We do not use deceptive headlines, auto-playing videos, or intrusive pop-ups. We believe the best way to earn your trust is to treat you like an intelligent adult.

Diversity of Voice: We actively seek out contributors from different regions, backgrounds, and viewpoints. The world is not one-dimensional, and neither is our coverage.

Why We Chose the Name "mega-store.in"

People often ask us about the name. No, we are not an e-commerce site. The name came from a late-night brainstorming session where someone joked that the internet had become a "mega store" of information, most of it low quality. We decided to reclaim that idea. If the web is a mega store of content, we want to be the section where you actually find something worth buying into. The ".in" reflects our roots, but our audience has long since gone global.

Looking Ahead

We are not pretending to be the biggest news site on the internet, and we are fine with that. Our goal for the next few years is simple: keep improving, keep hiring great journalists, and keep serving readers who care about quality. We are exploring new formats like long-form investigative pieces, podcast interviews, and regional language editions. But whatever we add, our core promise will not change: honest news, written by humans, for humans.

If you have made it this far down the page, thank you. Seriously. In a world of infinite scroll and eight-second attention spans, the fact that you took the time to read about who we are means a lot. We hope you will stick around, read a few articles, and let us know what you think.
